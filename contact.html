About me

My camera isn’t just a tool — it’s a compass, guiding me toward moments that can’t be staged, only witnessed. I’m drawn to the quiet truths of everyday life, the subtle shifts in expression or gesture that reveal entire worlds.

At the same time, I chase light. Not the obvious kind, but the light that shapes emotion — the kind that bends into something intimate, tactile, almost dreamlike. My process is instinctive and physical; I follow feeling first, image second. Every frame begins as an atmosphere before it becomes a picture.

And beneath it all, there’s discipline: the calm, the clarity, the devotion to a single image that carries the weight of a story. I’m drawn to compositions that breathe, to spaces that open up, to colors that don’t need to shout to be unforgettable.

My work lives in the space between observation and creation — between documentary honesty and poetic interpretation. I photograph and film to create closeness, not through spectacle, but through attention.

I believe in images that stay with you because they don’t explain — they resonate.
